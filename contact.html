Get in touch

You do not need to formulate the problem properly. Two sentences are enough — the rest is what the first session is for.

Your message has been sent.

I reply within one working day. If it feels urgent, please use the crisis contacts below rather than waiting for me.

Your message goes to me and to nobody else. It is not stored in any system beyond my mailbox, and I delete correspondence from people who do not become clients.

What happens next

I reply within a working day

With two or three possible times, and answers to anything you asked.

We meet for 50 minutes

Online or in person. €60. You decide afterwards whether to continue.

If it feels unbearable right now

Therapy is not emergency care and I cannot answer urgently. If you are thinking about harming yourself, please use one of these now, without waiting for an appointment. You do not have to explain yourself to them.

  • 112 — the emergency number across Europe and Georgia. Ask for medical or psychological help.
  • 24/7 crisis line — put your verified local number here. This demo deliberately does not print one, because a wrong number in this block is worse than none.
  • Someone nearby — a friend, a relative, a neighbour. Not being alone for the next few hours matters more than who exactly is with you.
